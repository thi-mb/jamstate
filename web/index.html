      Cues

      Keys 1–7 while you play. Breaks, build and take it down land on the next bar; the rest on the next phrase. The bandleader can't undo a cue for a phrase.

      Key 8 trades fours: play a phrase, lay out, and a band instrument answers in the next four bars; come back in and it hands back.

      Manual moves

      Go through the same governor as the bandleader.
