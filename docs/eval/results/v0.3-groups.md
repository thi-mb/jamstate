## What it says

- **Phrasing is the default.** It is the one group that helps or holds every
  brain: the rules gain the most (0.95 → 0.98, the phrasing scenarios
  0.68 → 0.96), Jev's phrasing scenarios go 0.68 → 0.70, Claude and Qwen hold.
- **All five on** is best for the rules and Jev (0.98, 0.89) but costs Qwen
  0.11 and doubles Claude's p95. Worth turning on with Jev or the rules.
- **Dynamics** alone hurts Claude and Qwen (0.84 each): they follow every swell
  and move too much in the "follow" scenarios. **Position** and **harmony** hurt
  Qwen. **Rhythm** changes nothing, as expected (the band keeps its tempo).
- Differences of 0.01 are inside the noise of 3 seeds.
- **Latency:** Jev stays far inside the budget with every group on. Qwen is
  over the budget already with none (2.3 s, the Spark serving the eval's
  other traffic at the same time) and gets slower with a bigger prompt;
  Claude goes over with all five. For Qwen and Claude keep the groups few.
