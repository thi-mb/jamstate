# v0.4 Leading: test plan

The bandleader leads across the whole song, not just phrase by phrase: each
genre has its own taste, you pick a personality, a section played again
differs from the last time through, and the band trades fours with you
([roadmap](../roadmap.md#v04-leading), [ADR-0021](../decisions/0021-leading-layer.md),
[ADR-0022](../decisions/0022-trading-fours.md),
[eval scenarios](../eval/scenarios.md#leading-v04)). Automated checks cover
everything that doesn't need hands; the rows under **Hands-on checks** are the playing
checks.

Result legend: ✅ pass · ⚠️ works but needs tuning · ❌ broken · ⏭ skipped

Signed off on 2026-11-14.
